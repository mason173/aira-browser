# Home Wallpaper Appearance Is Atomically Owned

Accepted: `HomeWallpaperCoordinator` is the single lifecycle owner for image-wallpaper appearance on native Browser Home. Wallpaper-dependent presentation is independent of App light/dark appearance, is persisted by image content, crop identity, layout identity when applicable, and algorithm version, and is published atomically with the wallpaper. Bing and custom wallpapers share this pipeline, while `BrowserShellPage` remains presentation wiring only. The durable target under this owner is layout-aware semantic-group analysis against the final composited background, with targeted local readability protection when neither black nor white alone is sufficient. Scrolling groups keep a stable presentation rather than recoloring continuously.

Current conservative base fallback: until the layout-aware group result is wired through Home presentation, the subordinate analyzer publishes one black-or-white Home Wallpaper Foreground Tone plus a subtle opposite-tone text readability shadow from the final masked Home Wallpaper Visible Crop; text-like symbols use the same tone without a component shadow. It defaults to light text and admits dark text only when mean relative luminance is above `0.70` and no more than `5%` of sampled pixels fall at or below `5.5:1` black-text contrast. These thresholds are Aira engineering defaults modeled on AOSP's configurable global wallpaper-hint defaults; they are not Huawei-confirmed launcher behavior and do not claim local contrast compliance. This replaces unsafe relative black-versus-white voting while preserving the future layout-aware owner boundary. Manual black/white controls, Home-runtime screenshots, cold-start analysis, and activation of an unanalyzed wallpaper remain rejected because they would introduce duplicate ownership, avoidable instability, or unnecessary work on startup.
